# Contributing

## IR Identity Changes

Changes affecting `deriveParent`, `hashNode`, or IR identity rules **must follow ADR-001**.

See [docs/adr/ADR-001-ir-identity-governance.md](docs/adr/ADR-001-ir-identity-governance.md)
for the Controlled Change Protocol.

Golden snapshot failures during controlled evolution are expected — they
are governance gates, not errors.
